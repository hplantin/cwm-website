Who we are

Contemplative community

Last summer I went on a Centering Prayer retreat organized by the Chicago chapter of Contemplative Outreach. I was impressed that that chapter has a newsletter listing area events of interest, holds meetings, organizes retreats, and offers fellowship and community.

Impressed and a little envious. I couldn't find anything similar in West Michigan.

I would like to start something similar in the Grand Rapids area. Contemplative West Michigan — CWM — is the beginning of that effort.

Did you know that cwm — pronounced "coom" — is a word? One of the very few in English where "w" is a vowel. A cwm is a small glacial valley head containing a lake: an area scraped clean by the insistent grinding of a glacier and filled with water. Often a place of austere beauty.

For now, CWM is an email list, a newsletter, and this web page — a clearinghouse for contemplative events and resources in West Michigan. In time, I hope it grows into something more.

Where we're headed

Growing into
something more

CWM begins simply — an email list and a webpage. But the hope is larger.

  • A calendar of contemplative events across West Michigan
  • Regular gatherings for fellowship, learning, and shared practice
  • An annual retreat or day of recollection
  • A small committee to help plan and run it all

If you'd like to help shape what CWM becomes, reply to any newsletter email or reach out directly. All are welcome.
