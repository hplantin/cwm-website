Who we are

Walking the inward
path, together

Contemplative West Michigan is a community for those drawn to the deeper dimensions of the spiritual life — centering prayer, contemplative reading, silence, and the slow work of inner transformation.

We gather monthly for reflection, learning, and shared practice. Whether you've been on this path for decades or are just beginning to sense its pull, you are welcome here.

Our meetings include a short talk or book discussion on a contemplative theme, time for conversation and fellowship, and a period of centering prayer or similar practice together.

Our meetings

What to expect
when we gather

Each monthly gathering weaves together three strands. Come for one, come for all — the rhythm is gentle and unhurried.

Learning

A book discussion or short talk exploring a contemplative theme — from the Desert Fathers to contemporary teachers like Thomas Keating or Cynthia Bourgeault.

Fellowship

Time for open conversation, sharing the joys and struggles of the inner life, and the simple gift of being in community with others who understand the journey.

Practice

A shared period of centering prayer or similar contemplative practice — sitting together in the silence that is beyond words, resting in God's presence.

Where we meet

In partnership with the
Dominican Center

We are grateful to partner with the Dominican Center for Spirituality in Grand Rapids. The Center brings a deep tradition of contemplative practice, a trusted reputation in the spiritual community, and a beautiful, intimate space for gatherings of up to 25.

Our meetings are free to attend and supported by the generosity of those who come. We believe the contemplative journey should never be gated by cost.

Where we're headed

Growing into
something more

We begin simply — a monthly gathering. But the hope is larger: a genuine community of practice, growing organically over time.

  • A leadership team drawn from the community itself
  • A newsletter for reflection, resources, and community news
  • Annual silent retreats and days of recollection
  • A home for those navigating the contemplative path in West Michigan
